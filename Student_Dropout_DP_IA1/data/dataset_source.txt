Ethical and Privacy Considerations:
Data originated from higher education administrative and academic records in Portugal. The dataset was sanitized by the original researchers to remove direct personal identifiers (names, student identification numbers, social security numbers) prior to public deposit.
However, because it contains granular demographic, socio-economic (e.g., parents' qualifications, scholarship status, debt status), and semester-by-semester academic performance, training a predictive model without privacy protections exposes records to membership inference attacks (MIA) and attribute disclosure.
This project applies Differentially Private Stochastic Gradient Descent (DP-SGD) to provide formal mathematical privacy guarantees against such attacks.
